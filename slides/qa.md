# Tough questions (answers must stay honest)

- **Why not Be My Eyes or Seeing AI?** They describe what the camera sees. Blindsight knows the building from CoStar data: room names, layout, what is around the corner before you arrive.
- **How accurate is it?** Quote only measured numbers recorded in NOTES-part2.md. Say the demo covers one scan point, the multi-pano graph is roadmap, and which numbers come from the simulated feed.
- **Tested with blind users?** Not yet. It is our first priority, and the cues follow established direction conventions. Do not claim more.
- **Privacy?** Frames are processed on our own laptop and not stored beyond local demo sessions. Say this only if recording is off in the demo.
- **How does this make CoStar money?** A differentiating feature on listing platforms that opens an underserved audience.
- **What is technically new?** Using an existing library of 3D scans as the localization map for assistive navigation, verified with geometry, instead of building new maps.
