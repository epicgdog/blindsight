# Blindsight pitch script (target under 3:00 at 150 words per minute)

> Stage notes and headings are not counted. Say only measured numbers. The only numbers here are counts taken from the scan itself. Slide numbers refer to slides/blindsight.pptx.

## 0:00 to 0:25 Problem (slides 1 and 2)
Blindsight lets blind and low-vision people tour a property independently, guided by voice, using CoStar 3D data.

If you cannot see, you cannot browse listings. Photos, floor plans and 3D tours all have to be looked at. And walking through an unfamiliar building alone is hard, so someone has to come with you.

## 0:25 to 0:50 Insight (slide 3)
CoStar already holds a detailed 3D record of a huge number of spaces. This building, Rabinowitz Hall, has 66 scan points on two floors, each one a 360 degree photo at a known position. It was built for sighted buyers. Nobody has turned it into guidance for people who cannot see.

## 0:50 to 2:00 Live demo (slide 4)
[Volunteer is blindfolded at the first taught place, holding the phone upright in portrait. One person talks, one drives the laptop, one guides the volunteer.]

Our volunteer cannot see. The phone streams its camera to our laptop. Listen.

[Location cue plays, for example: "View matches the front door."]

The laptop compared the live view with every scan of the building and confirmed the match with geometry. On screen you can see which scan it chose and how sure it is.

[Volunteer turns toward people or a bench. Cue: "2 people at 1 o'clock and a bench at 11 o'clock."]

That is object detection, spoken as clock directions.

[A teammate walks toward the volunteer. Cue: "Person directly ahead, getting closer."]

[Only if the voice agent is running: volunteer holds the talk button and asks "How do I get to the entrance?" The phone answers.]

A person who cannot see just learned where they are and what is around them, in real time.

## 2:00 to 2:35 How it works and roadmap (slides 5 to 7)
Phone video goes to the laptop. We detect objects, match the frame against the building's scans with geometric verification, and speak short cues back. Hold to talk, and a Gemini voice agent answers from what the system knows.

Next, scan points become a graph for room to room routes. Then Vexcel imagery guides someone from the street to the door, with a handoff to the indoor tour. That part is roadmap, not built. And before more features, our first priority is testing with blind users.

## 2:35 to 3:00 Why CoStar wins (slide 8)
This gives CoStar a new audience on its listing platforms, and a feature competitors cannot copy without this data.

Blindsight lets blind and low-vision people tour a property independently, guided by voice, using CoStar 3D data.
